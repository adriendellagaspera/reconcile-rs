    Architecture and limits

    Each replica embeds ReplicatedMap. reconcile-rs composes local writes, HLC/LWW merge, reconciliation and task lifecycle; RSOS / FingerprintTreeMap provides the ordered persistent tree and range aggregates; RBSR compares ranges; gossip supplies authenticated messaging and discovery. RSOS is inside every replica, not a central server.

    The displayed values, stamps and summaries come from actual Rust snapshots. Convergence is checked by exact dated-entry equality, not fingerprint equality alone. The greatest HLC + node stamp wins a same-key conflict; the other value is discarded. This is eventual consistency, not consensus, exclusive ownership or business conflict resolution.

    Connectivity is controlled by an in-memory transport decorator. No physical network, artificial packet animation, persistence, or performance claim is demonstrated. There are three keys: this explains the mechanism, not its scaling. Periodic protocol traffic continues after convergence.
